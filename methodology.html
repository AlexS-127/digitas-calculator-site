Integration Cost Calculator

Formulas, defaults and sources.

Every number in the calculator comes from your inputs or from the defaults below. Each default is marked sourced (from a published study), derived (computed from sourced figures) or assumed (our estimate, to replace with your own).

What is compared

A client working with N specialist agencies, one per discipline, against one integrated partner covering the same scope, over a horizon of H years. Fees are assumed equal unless you change the fee difference, so the gap comes from the costs around the fees.

A relationship that lasts T years on average ends at a rate of 1 ÷ T per year (steady state, our derivation). Three specialists in creative, media and CRM (6.75, 5.94 and 6.29 years) make about 4.8 switches in 10 years; one integrated partner at 7.96 years makes about 1.3. The chart spaces switches evenly at that average rate; real ones are lumpier.

Specialist tenure by discipline

Client-reported average tenure of agency-of-record relationships, by agency type (ANA survey of 106 client-side members, fielded 2024). Where our discipline has no matching agency type, the mapping is our assumption.

What it leaves out

  • Performance: integrated work may also perform better (IPA). The calculator counts costs only.
  • Fees by scope: Digitas does not share rate cards, so fees are your input, not a benchmark.
  • One client's experience can differ a lot from industry averages. Use your own tenure and review costs where you know them.
