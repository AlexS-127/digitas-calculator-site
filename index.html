Integration Cost Calculator

Specialist agencies cost more than their fees

This calculator compares several specialist agencies with one integrated partner over 5 to 15 years. It counts what fees miss: replacing agencies more often, paying two agencies during each handover, and your team's time keeping them coordinated.

What the calculator counts

Agency reviews

What you spend running a pitch each time you replace an agency. Specialists get replaced more often, so you pay for more pitches.

Coordination time

Your own team's hours managing each agency and the handoffs between them, valued at your staff's hourly cost.

Handover overlap

Paying the old and the new agency at the same time while you switch from one to the other.
